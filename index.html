Delivery

Choose the delivery term

Every shipment is agreed as FOB, CFR or CIF Chattogram.

FOB: Free On Board

The seller delivers the cotton on board the vessel at the U.S. port of loading. The buyer arranges and pays for ocean freight and insurance to Chattogram.

Suits buyers who want control of freight.

CFR: Cost and Freight

The seller arranges and pays ocean freight to Chattogram. Risk passes to the buyer once the cotton is loaded. The buyer arranges insurance.

Suits buyers who want a delivered-freight price.

CIF: Cost, Insurance and Freight

As CFR, and the seller also buys minimum insurance cover to Chattogram for the buyer's benefit. Risk still passes on loading.

Suits buyers who want freight and insurance included.

Final Incoterms and payment terms are agreed per transaction.
